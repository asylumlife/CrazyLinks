# Self-hosted link pages exploit Linktree's paywalls

The link-in-bio market is a rental market: Linktree and its forty-odd SaaS rivals give away a basic list of links and then charge monthly, or take a cut of sales, for the parts creators care about most: their own domain, removing the vendor's branding, analytics history beyond a few weeks, styling, tracking pixels, email capture and fee-free selling. Linktree's top tier is **$35/month**. Its Starter and Pro prices went up **46-67% in November 2025** and may have risen again in late October 2026. Commerce rivals take **8-12% of sales on free plans**. WordPress has no strong answer yet. The most-installed link-in-bio plugin has **about 2,000 installs**, and its reviews and those of its nearest rival complain about the same two things: basics locked behind Pro, and permalink 404s. User complaints across platforms are mostly about money and trust (surprise billing, slow payouts, unresponsive support, opaque bans, products shut down after acquisitions), not missing features. A self-hosted plugin removes rent, fees and shutdown risk by design. Two outside constraints shape the page. Visitors land in Instagram and TikTok in-app browsers, which break app deep links and cookie-based analytics. And WordPress 7.0 (May 2026) shipped a provider-agnostic AI Client, Connectors and an Abilities API, with an MCP Adapter plugin on top, so "edit my page with AI" can be built on core APIs rather than a proprietary proxy. The evidence has weak spots. Linktree's own USD prices could not be read. Reddit, G2 and Capterra were blocked. Instagram and TikTok help pages did not render. Confidence labels below mark which facts are safe to build on.

**Confidence key used throughout.** **High**: read from a first-party source (vendor help or pricing page, official docs, wordpress.org listing). **Medium**: several consistent secondary sources, or one first-party source that is partial. **Low**: a single secondary or vendor-authored source, or sources that conflict. Most "best alternatives" roundups cited here are written by competing vendors and rank themselves first.

## Linktree charges up to $35 a month, and its own price list is contested

Linktree has four tiers (Free, Starter, Pro, Premium). Its help center gives the clearest primary evidence of what each tier unlocks. Every paid tier removes ads and adds thumbnails, link scheduling, Spotlight/priority animation, Redirect, Profile Lock, QR scan counts and up to 365 days of per-link insights. **Pro and above** unlock almost all design depth and all marketing plumbing: custom background images and video, fonts, hiding the Linktree logo, QR customization, Instagram layouts, RSS previews, app-store routing, music pre-save, Code Lock, media kit, polls and surveys, phone capture, subscriber CSV export, Mailchimp/Klaviyo/Kit/beehiiv/Flodesk/Google Sheets integrations, Meta Pixel, GA4, UTM parameters, custom meta title and description, and selling digital products and courses. **Premium alone** adds lifetime insights with CSV download, personalized links per visitor, "AI tools" and Admins ([Linktree help: paid features](https://linktr.ee/help/articles/5434140-an-overview-of-paid-features-available-on-linktree)) (High). Analytics cover views, clicks and per-link performance, with CSV export of cities, countries, devices and referrers ([Linktree Insights](https://linktr.ee/help/collections/7058283-understanding-your-analytics)). The Meta Pixel supports the Conversions API via access token ([Linktree Meta Pixel help](https://linktr.ee/help/en/articles/5434194-facebook-pixel-integration)) (High).

Linktree's commerce layer grew in 2025. It added digital products, Kajabi-powered courses, bookings, US-only affiliate Shops and US-only sponsored links capped at three live ([Linktree Shops](https://linktr.ee/features/shops), [sponsored links help](https://linktr.ee/help/articles/11006101-how-to-earn-with-sponsored-links-on-your-linktree), [SmartCompany](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/technology/linktree-new-ai-features-deeper-canva-integration/)). Its growth strategy is to buy competitors and close them. **Koji** was acquired and shut on 1 February 2024 ([Koji shutdown](https://withkoji.com/koji-shutdown)). **Bento** closed on 13 February 2026, its URLs now redirect to Linktree and its data was deleted ([Own.page](https://own.page/blog/bento-alternatives)). **Fingertip** closed around the end of April 2026 ([SmartCompany](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/startupsmart/linktree-acquires-leibovich-brothers-backed-fingertip-to-shut-it-down/)). Linktree reportedly has 70M+ users ([Built In](https://builtin.com/company/linktree/faq/stability-growth)) (Medium).

A single-URL slot on TikTok, X and Pinterest is the structural reason link-in-bio pages exist. The click then lands somewhere hostile. Instagram and Facebook on iOS open links in their own webview. In 2022 Felix Krause showed that Instagram injects Meta's `pcm.js` into third-party pages, and Meta called it intentional ([krausefx.com](https://krausefx.com/blog/ios-privacy-instagram-and-facebook-can-track-anything-you-do-on-any-website-in-their-in-app-browser)) (High for 2022; current behavior unverified). Google's own documentation says Android WebViews "don't share state with the browser", so visitors are logged out of everything, and cookies and saved payment methods are missing ([Chrome Custom Tabs](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/android/custom-tabs)) (High). App Links and Universal Links are system routing features. The docs say nothing about third-party webviews, and secondary sources report they usually fail to launch native apps from inside Instagram or TikTok ([developer.android.com](https://developer.android.com/training/app-links/about), [Linkrunner](https://linkrunner.io/blog/universal-links-app-links-break-in-app-browsers)) (Medium). There is no documented way to force an exit to the system browser. `x-safari-https://`, `instagram://extbrowser` and Android `intent://` URLs are undocumented, with mixed results ([plugwith.me](https://plugwith.me/blog/what-escapes-instagram-in-app-browser-in-2026/), [Apple developer forum](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/757541)) (Low). In-app-browser user-agent tokens (`Instagram`, `FBAN/FBAV`, `BytedanceWebview`, `musical_ly`) are reported but not guaranteed by the apps, and some iOS 26 Facebook builds reportedly omit them ([gotoapp.store](https://gotoapp.store/blog/detect-in-app-browser)) (Low). Google sign-in fails in embedded webviews with `disallowed_useragent` ([truelink-group](https://truelink-group.com/en/blog/why-google-login-fails-in-line-facebook-in-app-browsers-2026/)) (Medium).

Analytics suffer the same way. Fresh webview sessions strip referrers and reset `_ga` IDs, so GA4 reports Direct traffic and inflated new users, and UTM parameters are the documented fix ([Rule Analytics](https://www.ruleranalytics.com/blog/social-media/track-instagram-traffic/)) (Medium). Meta publishes no guidance on Pixel behavior inside its own in-app browsers, and its Pixel Helper, now "Meta Ads Data Advisor", is a desktop-only extension ([Meta Ads Data Advisor](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/meta-pixel/support/pixel-helper/)) (High). Performance targets come from Core Web Vitals at the 75th percentile: LCP ≤2.5 s, INP ≤200 ms, CLS ≤0.1 ([web.dev](https://web.dev/articles/vitals)) (High). The LCP image should be discoverable in the initial HTML, use `fetchpriority="high"` and never be lazy-loaded ([web.dev Optimize LCP](https://web.dev/articles/optimize-lcp)) (Medium). No official Meta or TikTok statement names link-in-bio or shortener domains as throttled. Meta's spam policy covers cloaking and deceptive redirects, but how links are scored is not published ([AwareXone](https://www.awarexone.com/research/instagram-review-flagged-links-warning)) (Low). Web NFC works only in Chrome for Android, so NFC tags should carry a plain https URL ([Chrome Web NFC](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/capabilities/nfc)) (High).

## WordPress 7.0 put a provider-agnostic AI client in core

Hosted rivals already sell AI editing. **Linktree Premium** lets users connect Claude or ChatGPT through an MCP server (`https://mcp.linktr.ee/mcp`). The agent can add, edit, reorder and delete links, change bio and appearance, and read Insights. It acts with the user's permissions, cannot see payment or visitor data, and its changes are logged separately from human admin changes ([Linktree help: AI tools](https://linktr.ee/help/articles/15551042-manage-linktree-ai-tools)) (High). Linktree also has AI restyle and layout suggestions with Canva (Sept 2025), AI Insights Chat, and a 5 July 2026 terms update that reportedly allows sharing inputs with model providers ([SmartCompany](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/technology/linktree-new-ai-features-deeper-canva-integration/), [Linke.ro](https://linke.ro/blog/linktree-ai-training-policy-2026-explained)) (Medium; verify the terms). **Beacons** meters AI by daily credits: 30/day on Free, including 1 "AI Link in Bio Designer" use ([Beacons pricing](https://beacons.ai/i/pricing)) (High). Its setup wizard reportedly builds a page from a social handle ([autogpt.net](https://autogpt.net/ai-tool/beacons-ai/)) (Low). Taplink generates a page in about 10 seconds on all plans ([Taplink blog](https://taplink.at/en/blog/taplink-ai-link-in-bio-tool.html)). Wix Harmony (January 2026) and Squarespace Blueprint AI set the bar for prompt-built sites ([PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/01/21/wix-harmony-lets-you-build-a-website-using-natural-language-prompts/), [Squarespace](https://www.squarespace.com/blog/blueprint-ai-website-builder)). Stan and Carrd show little AI.

Inside WordPress, **WordPress 7.0 shipped on 20 May 2026** with the AI Client, a Connectors screen where admins add provider keys (Anthropic, Google and OpenAI by default), and the client-side Abilities API. The PHP Abilities registry had already landed in 6.9 ([InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/07/wordpress-7-ai/), [Make Core: client-side abilities](https://make.wordpress.org/core/2026/03/24/client-side-abilities-api-in-wordpress-7-0/)) (High). The developer entry point is `wp_ai_client_prompt()`, a fluent builder with `generate_text()` and `generate_image()`. Calls return `WP_Error` on failure, and `is_supported_for_text_generation()` checks availability without making an API call. Core bundles no providers. A `wp_ai_client_prevent_prompt` filter can block prompts. The dev note tells plugin authors to build narrow REST endpoints with their own permission checks, because the core client has **no built-in rate limit or cost cap** ([Make Core dev note](https://make.wordpress.org/core/2026/03/24/introducing-the-ai-client-in-wordpress-7-0/)) (High). The **MCP Adapter** plugin (v0.7.1, 16 Oct 2026, WP 6.9+) exposes registered abilities to external agents through `discover-abilities`, `get-ability-info` and `execute-ability`. Abilities stay private unless flagged public, and readonly, destructive and idempotent annotations map to MCP hints ([Make AI](https://make.wordpress.org/ai/2026/10/07/the-mcp-adapter-plugin-is-now-available-on-wordpress-org/), [wordpress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/mcp-adapter/)) (High). The optional AI Experiments plugin, being renamed "WordPress AI", reached 0.4.x in March 2026 ([Make AI](https://make.wordpress.org/ai/2026/03/06/whats-new-in-ai-experiments-0-4-0/)). WordPress 7.1 was reported due on 19 August 2026 ([nexterwp](https://nexterwp.com/blog/wordpress-7-ai/)). A plugin listing "tested to WP 7.1.4" suggests it shipped ([wp.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/social-lite/)) (Medium). It is still unknown whether 7.1 changed the AI APIs, whether the AI Client supports streaming, and how Connectors stores keys.

Existing plugins show two integration models. **AI Engine** (v3.9.0, 2 Nov 2026) uses the site owner's own provider keys across nine providers plus local OpenAI-compatible endpoints. It runs an MCP server with approval gates and a draft-only mode, edits Gutenberg content block by block, and had to fix an MCP path that **leaked API keys through option reads** ([wordpress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/ai-engine/)) (High). **Elementor** and **Jetpack** route AI through their own servers and sell credits: Elementor One includes 25,000 credits/month at €180/yr, and Jetpack gives 20 free requests ([Elementor](https://elementor.com/blog/ai-wordpress-plugin/), [themeisle](https://themeisle.com/blog/best-wordpress-ai-plugins/)) (Medium). The wordpress.org guidelines have no AI-specific rule, but the general rules bind. Guideline 5 bans locking plugin code behind payment, trials or quotas. Guideline 6 requires any SaaS to do substantive work and be documented in the readme. Guideline 7 requires explicit opt-in before contacting external servers. Guideline 8 bans loading non-service JS and CSS from outside hosts ([Detailed plugin guidelines](https://developer.wordpress.org/plugins/wordpress-org/detailed-plugin-guidelines/)) (High). On cost, Anthropic's pricing page lists Sonnet 5.5 at $2/$10 and Haiku 5.5 at $0.10/$0.50 per million input/output tokens (Haiku's rate applies to prompts up to 100K tokens) ([Anthropic pricing](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing)) (High). Structured outputs guarantee schema-valid JSON but reject recursive schemas and require `additionalProperties: false` ([Structured outputs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/structured-outputs)) (High). At those prices, an edit that sends about 5K tokens and returns 1K costs about $0.02 on Sonnet 5.5 and about $0.001 on Haiku 5.5 (arithmetic from the listed prices). It is unverified whether the WordPress AI Client maps its JSON-schema helper to Anthropic's native structured outputs.

## Implications for a WordPress plugin

Each row ties a product requirement to the evidence above. None of these is new speculation. Where the evidence is thin, the confidence column says so.

|---|---|---|
| Ship free, in core, the features hosted tools sell as upgrades: no branding, own domain, unlimited analytics history with CSV export, pixels/GA4/UTM, email capture, scheduling | Linktree gates all of these at Pro/Premium; paywalled basics rank #2 among pains; wp.org Guideline 5 forbids locking plugin code anyway | High |
| Avoid aggressive in-dashboard upsells; keep basic styling (colors, fonts, backgrounds) free | The Link in Bio Creator 1-star review and "Limited" review title; Guideline 5 | High |

## Conclusion

The evidence changes what "replacing Linktree" means. The fight is not mainly about block types, since nearly every tool already offers links, embeds, QR and basic analytics. It is about the business model. Users' loudest complaints are rent, fees, surprise billing, held payouts, opaque moderation and shutdowns, and self-hosting removes each of them by design rather than by out-featuring anyone. Meanwhile the WordPress plugins that should have owned this advantage undercut it with upsell walls and rewrite-rule bugs. The plugin can lose that opportunity in two ways: by recreating SaaS-style gating inside wp-admin, or by shipping a heavy page that runs slowly in the Instagram webview where most visits happen.

On AI, timing favors WordPress. Linktree keeps agent editing behind a $35 Premium plan. WordPress core now provides a provider-agnostic client, a permissions-aware Abilities registry and an MCP bridge, so the same capability can ship free, run on the site owner's own key, and work from both an in-admin prompt box and external agents. The open questions to close before writing the spec are mostly verification tasks: Linktree's current USD prices and fee schedule, Instagram's and TikTok's official link rules, whether the AI Client supports streaming and native structured output, and first-hand Reddit and G2 data that would show which features (not just which frustrations) users want most.
