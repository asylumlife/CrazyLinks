## What do WordPress link-in-bio plugin users complain about or request?

### Takeaway
The main complaints are paywalled basic styling in Link in Bio Creator and permalink 404s in WP MyLinks. Requests are mostly small embed and field features. Volume is small and most threads are old.

### Cited Findings
Link in Bio Creator – Social (slug social-lite), https://wordpress.org/support/plugin/social-lite/ . 21 ratings: 16 five-star, 2 three-star, 1 two-star, 2 one-star.
- ebarcena, review "Extremely gated to force an upgrade", about Dec 2025 (relative date, not exact): "almost anything you click in the settings prompts you to upgrade to Pro" and "You can't even change the background color." Plans to build their own link-in-bio page. https://wordpress.org/support/topic/extremely-gated-to-force-an-upgrade/
- Review titles only (body not retrieved): "Limited" (zeuskorleone), "SLOW RESPONSE TIME" (wittyai), "All titles become the same." (Ewot), "Hard to use" (ebuege). https://wordpress.org/support/plugin/social-lite/reviews/
- Support thread by schmekbu, about mid-2024: "I wanted to bring to your attention an issue with the redirection from Instagram and Facebook when using mobile devices." Developer found root cause; fixed in 1.5.9. https://wordpress.org/support/topic/404-not-found-link-is-not-working-if-coming-from-facebook-or-instagram/
- Thread titles: "React, React DOM and MobX loaded globally on every frontend page" (etienneh, about Oct 2026, performance); "Update/sync social links & analytics across sites" (ipanis, multi-site sync request); "Live links page not matching preview"; "Members can create profiles with links ?" (user-generated pages request); "Site similar to allmylinks". Bodies not retrieved.

WP MyLinks, https://wordpress.org/support/plugin/wp-mylinks/ . 14 ratings: 10 five-star, 1 three-star, 3 one-star.
- Alex De Py, review "Solid link hub plugin, but rewrite conflict and JS workarounds hold it back" (3 stars, about mid-2025, no exact date): no built-in webfont field, needs custom JavaScript to send Google Tag Manager click events (analytics request), and a broad rewrite rule causes 404s. https://wordpress.org/support/topic/solid-link-hub-plugin-but-rewrite-conflict-and-js-workarounds-hold-it-back/
- Support thread titles (bodies not retrieved, mostly 2020-2023): permalink and 404 errors (several: "Permalinks Error", "404 On Latest Update", "404 Errors Caused by Unscoped Rewrite Rule"); "Feature Request: Content Publication/Expiration Date" (link scheduling); "Feature Request: Search Bar"; "FontAwesome Icon Support"; "Spotify Embed"; "Add Discord Social Link"; "Headings"; "HTML Block"; "Youtube embed"; "Change mylink slug"; "Multisite Compatible".
- Review titles: "V1.0.6 has broken my woocommerce" (yepyip), "Poorly Coded" (feliks), "Super Equivalent to Linktr" (devidia).

### Inferences
- For WordPress plugins the demand signals are: free tier too limited (Link in Bio Creator), reliability and permalink conflicts (WP MyLinks), tag-manager-style click tracking, expiry dates on links.

### Gaps
- Individual star ratings and most review and thread bodies were not retrieved (page shows titles only).
- "Link in Bio Creator – Social" is slug social-lite; the slug link-in-bio-creator 404s.

## Feature request evidence

### Takeaway
Direct user evidence is thin because Reddit, G2 and Capterra were not retrievable. Only the items below have any evidence.

### Cited Findings
- Link scheduling and expiry: one WP MyLinks thread, "Feature Request: Content Publication/Expiration Date" (title only), https://wordpress.org/support/plugin/wp-mylinks/
- Per-link analytics and tracking: Alex De Py needs custom JS to send GTM click events (WP MyLinks, link above); Link in Bio Creator user wants analytics synced across sites (ipanis thread title).
- Custom domain: Small Creator, Beacons, Jan 2026, "Domain ownership is misleading." Shopify blog (vendor, low weight) says Linktree has no custom domains: https://www.shopify.com/blog/linktree-alternatives
- Custom styling or background colour: ebarcena (Link in Bio Creator) complains it is paywalled.
- Multiple pages and user-generated pages: "Members can create profiles with links ?" and "Site similar to allmylinks" (Link in Bio Creator threads, titles only).
- Embeds (Spotify, YouTube, Discord, FontAwesome icons, HTML block): WP MyLinks request threads (titles only).
- Single-transaction cash-out: Rodlande, Stan Trustpilot, 16 Jul 2026.
- No evidence found (in retrieved sources) for: A/B testing, deep links that open native apps, UTM tagging, email-list sync, team access, QR codes, thumbnails, video backgrounds, pinned links, countdown timers, password-protected links, geo-targeting, data export. This reflects blocked sources, not absence of demand.

### Inferences
- Do not treat the "no evidence" list as demand being low.

### Gaps
- Needs Reddit, G2, Capterra, Product Hunt access to quantify.
