# Link-in-bio SaaS competitors to Linktree (state as of 2026-11-05)

Method note: ~28 web searches/fetches. Prices are USD, recorded 2026-11-05 unless dated otherwise. Most "best alternatives" roundups are published by competing vendors (brandID, Linke.ro, atom.bio, Replug, InstantDM, Taplink, etc.) and rank themselves first; treat their claims as marketing. Official pages were retrievable only for Beacons, Carrd, Komi, Later, Taplink (partial). Stan, Snipfeed, Flowpage, Hopp pricing pages were 404, redirect, gated or empty.

## 1. Per-tool profiles (audience, pricing, free limits, standouts, weaknesses, status)

### Takeaway
The market is splitting into (a) commerce-first storefronts (Stan, Beacons, Komi, Hopp, Instabio), (b) design/website-lite builders (Carrd, Milkshake, Taplink, Campsite), (c) marketer/agency tools (Shorby, Replug, Taplink Business), (d) social-suite add-ons (Later, Pallyy, Planoly), and (e) music-specific smart links (Linkfire, Feature.fm). Several products are DEAD (Linkpop, Koji, Bento, Snipfeed standalone, Tap Bio, kit.co) so product-survival risk is itself a market theme.

## 2. Table stakes vs differentiators

### Takeaway
Table stakes (nearly universal): unlimited links, themes/templates, mobile-responsive page, social-icon links, media embeds (YouTube/Spotify), QR code, basic click analytics, tip jar/payment link, free tier (except Stan, Later, Shorby, Linkfire, Liinks). Differentiators: native courses/memberships, bookings, email/SMS, AutoDM, pixels with A/B testing, multi-page sites, white-label, team seats, AI page generation.

### Cited Findings
- Unlimited links, themes, basic analytics, embeds, QR are listed as free-tier features by atom.bio for nearly every tool (Bio.link, Beacons, Taplink, Campsite, Lnk.Bio, Hopp etc.) — [atom.bio](https://www.atom.bio/en/linktree-alternatives/)
- Tipping/donations: Beacons, Lnk.Bio (fee-free), AllMyLinks (90% payout), Hoo.be, Campsite (tips only), Bio Sites — [Bitly](https://bitly.com/blog/linktree-alternatives/), [atom.bio](https://www.atom.bio/en/linktree-alternatives/). Absent: Tap Bio, Liinks.
- Free-forever-plan absence: Stan, Later, Shorby, Linkfire, Leadpages, Sked Link, Liinks (trial only) — see section 1.
- Rare features: Pre-Rolls (Hopp only per Linke.ro, vendor claim); built-in search (Hopp); swipeable story cards (Milkshake, Tap Bio); brand-deal marketplace with agentic outreach (Komi); AdSense "Earn" (HeyLink); NFC card (Beacons Max); link routing/rules (Linktree Premium); IG feed-to-tile (Later); smart pre-save links (Feature.fm/Linkfire); dynamic QR routing (Flowpage, Bitly); bundled social scheduling (Later, Pallyy, Buffer, Linktree Premium).

### Inferences
- Table-stakes bar is rising: custom domain on a cheap paid tier (Beacons $10, Komi $16, Carrd $19/yr, Liinks $12), 0% fee, and email capture are becoming expected.

### Gaps
- No tool-by-tool verified feature checklist from official docs for most vendors; table-stakes assessment relies on roundups.

## 3. Monetization and commerce

### Takeaway
Digital products and tips are near-universal on commerce tools; courses/memberships, bookings and email are concentrated in Beacons, Stan, Hopp, Komi, Taplink Business, brandID. Paid DMs, invoicing: little evidence found.

### Cited Findings
- Digital products: Beacons (unlimited, all tiers), Stan, Komi Pro, Taplink Business, Instabio, Hopp, Lnk.Bio Unique, Linktree Pro+ — section 1 sources.
- Courses: Beacons (1 free, unlimited with video on Plus), Stan, Hopp, The Leap — [Beacons pricing](https://beacons.ai/i/pricing), [Linke.ro Hopp](https://linke.ro/blog/hopp-by-wix-review)
- Memberships/subscriptions: Beacons Plus (unlimited), Hopp, brandID — same sources.
- Bookings: Stan (Creator tier), Hopp (native), Julip, brandID — same.
- Affiliate: Beacons (keeps 100% commission on Plus), Komi, brandID marketplace of 5M products, Stan Pro (affiliate management for sellers), Hopp (affiliate links + dropshipping).
- Email/SMS: Beacons (email sends capped by tier), Stan Pro, Komi Pro (email) and email/SMS marketing (VidPros), Hopp (email, CRM, automations), Linktree Pro (integrations only), Lnk.Bio Unique (newsletter forms).
- Instagram AutoDM: Stan (Creator), Linktree (limited free, unlimited Premium). Third-party tool InstantDM sells this separately.
- BNPL: Beacons Plus. Payment plans: Stan Pro. Event tickets, physical goods with inventory: Hopp.
- Fees: see section 1 (Beacons 9% free/Creator, 0% Plus; Stan 0% + Stripe; Instabio 10/6/3%; Milkshake 12%; Bio Sites 8%; HeyLink 9.9%+30c free; Komi/Hopp/Linktree Pro 0%).
- No evidence found of: paid DMs / paid messaging or invoicing as a feature of any tool in scope.

### Inferences
- Stan's moat is workflow (AutoDM + bookings + courses) rather than page design; Beacons is the broadest at the low end.

### Gaps
- Paid DMs and invoicing: searched implicitly, not found; may exist in unreviewed tools (e.g. Fanfix-style products).

## 5. Analytics and tracking

### Takeaway
Basic click/view counts are universal; pixels and GA are mid-tier; A/B testing, heatmaps and conversion attribution are rare and found mainly at Replug, Leadpages, Sked Link, Shorby (retargeting).

### Cited Findings
- Basic analytics free: Linktree (28 days), Campsite (14 days), Milkshake (30 days), Beacons, Komi, Bio.link, Lnk.Bio, Hopp.
- Retention tiers: Linktree 28d/90d/365d/all-time; Later 1 yr/2 yr; Campsite 14d to 60d; Feature.fm 7-day free — sources above.
- Pixels/GA: Meta Pixel (Komi Pro, Taplink Pro, Stan Pro "ad pixel", Linktree Pro), 7 ad platforms (Shorby Pro), GA4 (Beacons), Campsite Pro pixels, Instabio Premium pixels, Feature.fm CAPI, Lnk.Bio FB/TikTok pixel (disputed).
- Link scheduling: Campsite Pro, Taplink Pro (scheduled blocks), Lnk.Bio Unique, Linktree (scheduling), Replug (per ClickUp "LinkPilot" scheduled links).
- A/B testing: Replug, Leadpages — [brandID](https://brandid.app/blog/best-link-in-bio-tools/), [Bitly](https://bitly.com/blog/linktree-alternatives/). Automatic UTM: Sked Link — [ClickUp](https://clickup.com/blog/link-in-bio-tools/).
- Heatmaps: none found in any tool reviewed. Conversion/revenue tracking: Stan (income tracking), Beacons (store analytics). Page view by location: Later.

### Inferences
- Analytics depth is the most common upsell lever (Linktree, Campsite, Feature.fm all gate history length).

### Gaps
- UTM support, heatmaps, and conversion funnels unverified for most tools.

## 6. Custom domains, SEO, multi-page, team seats, white-label, API

### Takeaway
