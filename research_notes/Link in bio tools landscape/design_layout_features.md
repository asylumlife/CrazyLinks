# Visual design, image and layout capabilities of link-in-bio tools (state as of Nov 2026)

Method note: ~28 searches/fetches. Official help pages were used where reachable (Linktree, Beacons, Wix, Carrd). Many vendor pages (Taplink, Komi, Lnk.Bio, Campsite, Liinks, Later, Milkshake, Stan, Bento) were only reachable through search summaries or competitor/reviewer blogs, which are marked [R] (reviewer/third party, often a competing vendor) vs [O] (official). Dates are retrieval date 2026-11-05 unless stated. Search-snippet summaries were not independently verified against full pages.

## Page layouts (list, grid, featured, carousel, cards, bento, tabs, IG feed, stickers)

### Takeaway
Linktree remains a vertical list with per-link Classic/Featured display plus Instagram Grid/Cards/Carousel and (since June 2026) stickers and new header layouts. True bento grids came from Bento.me (closed 13 Feb 2026), now copied by many small vendors. Swipeable cards (Milkshake), tabs/multi-page (Hopp, Beacons, Liinks), and feed-to-tiles (Later) are the other distinct models.

### Cited Findings
- Linktree "Featured layout" (launched 20 Mar 2024): a link expands into a large visual preview with interactive previews for YouTube, TikTok, Spotify, Instagram, stores; per link choose "Featured" (content preview + call to action) or "Classic" (compact button) in the Display section [O] — [Linktree blog](https://linktr.ee/blog/big-bold-and-visual-four-new-features-that-just-dropped-on-linktree); [Linktree help](https://linktr.ee/help/articles/8580581-how-to-feature-your-links-with-a-visual-preview-on-linktree)
- Featured Layout plan limits: Free 1 link; Starter/Pro/Premium unlimited [O] — [Linktree paid features](https://linktr.ee/help/en/articles/5434140-an-overview-of-paid-features-available-on-linktree)
- Featured thumbnail: 16:9, min 680x383 px; Classic thumbnail: 1:1, min 200x200 px. Sources for thumbnails: upload, Canva, GIPHY GIFs, AI-generated, Tabler icons [O] — [Linktree help](https://linktr.ee/help/articles/8580581-how-to-feature-your-links-with-a-visual-preview-on-linktree); [thumbnail help](https://linktr.ee/help/articles/5434160-add-thumbnail-links)
- Linktree "Instagram layouts": connect a professional IG account; shows six latest posts/reels (latest, most popular, or hand-picked). Layout options Carousel, Profile (posts + follower count + Follow button), Single post = Pro/Premium; Classic, Cards, Grid also appear as options [O] — [Linktree help](https://linktr.ee/help/en/articles/8629127-display-instagram-posts-linktree)
- Linktree Text Links (Mar 2024): blocks of text directly on the page (longer bio, opening hours, blog preview) [O] — [Linktree blog](https://linktr.ee/blog/big-bold-and-visual-four-new-features-that-just-dropped-on-linktree)
- Linktree Image Gallery link: 3 images on Free/Starter, unlimited on Pro/Premium; also FAQ link (5 questions Free/Starter) [O] — [Linktree paid features](https://linktr.ee/help/en/articles/5434140-an-overview-of-paid-features-available-on-linktree)
- Linktree "Sticker Bio Boards": launched 15 Jun 2026 with new header layouts; stickers placed on the page as a visual layer over the card-based profile; first pack tied to Olivia Rodrigo album; future themed drops planned. Plan tiers, sticker limits and header-layout details NOT published in coverage found; one guide warns sticker rendering can shift between desktop and mobile [R] — [DesignRush](https://news.designrush.com/olivia-rodrigo-linktree-bio-stickers); [linke.ro](https://linke.ro/blog/linktree-sticker-bio-boards-olivia-rodrigo-explained). Linktree's own Design help page lists "Stickers" as a Design-tab section alongside Theme, Header, Wallpaper, Buttons, Text, Enhance [O] — [Linktree help](https://linktr.ee/help/articles/8614125-customize-your-linktree-appearance)
- Bento.me: closed 13 Feb 2026; reportedly acquired by Linktree and URL redirects to Linktree [R, vendor-written] — [ManyLinks](https://www.manylinks.io/bento-alternative); [Podmu/others via search](https://podmu.com/blog/bento-me-shutting-down-alternatives). Described as the "visual-grid leader"; replacements advertise bento grid layouts, image blocks, live-data widgets (ManyLinks 20+), one-click Bento import (ManyLinks, Taap.bio) [R]
- Milkshake: mobile-only editor; page is a sequence of swipeable Cards (story-like); card types link list, About, YouTube embed, Top Picks, Splash (quote/image); cards hold text, images, GIFs, videos, podcast episodes, contact details, links; up to 5 separate sites per account [R] — [TheLeap](https://www.theleap.co/blog/linktree-vs-milkshake/); [Liink](https://liink.is/blog/best-link-in-bio-tools)
- Beacons: drag-and-drop blocks (links, images, video, music, forms, products, custom HTML); header block has 4 layout types / 12 options; navigation via tabs on top or hamburger menu; store pop-up; Classic links mode with collapsible individual links; separate website builder with multi-page, pop-ups, hero/content/media sections [O for header/nav/collapsible: [Beacons design help](https://help.beacons.ai/en/articles/4697153), [Links block](https://help.beacons.ai/en/articles/4696577)]; [R for blocks and site builder: [Taplink blog](https://taplink.at/en/blog/what-is-beacons-ai.html)]
- Hopp by Wix: multiple pages/categories, in-page search bar, social icons, designer-made layouts; some layouts free, premium layouts and interactive-button range paid; Blank template has 3 layouts (profile photo, social icons, dropdown list of links) [O: [Wix support](https://support.wix.com/en/article/hopp-by-wix-an-overview)]; [R: [Hopp review summary](https://linke.ro/blog/hopp-by-wix-review)]
- Later Link in Bio: mirrors Instagram feed as clickable tiles, each post can link to its own URL (reported up to 5 links per post per one source), plus link buttons and featured media (latest post enlarged); TikTok posts linkable; bannerless page and custom domain on Starter and up (Starter price disputed $18 vs $25/mo) [R] — [Later help](https://help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/4409203074967-What-is-Link-in-Bio); [BloggingWizard](https://bloggingwizard.com/instagram-bio-link-tools/)
- Liinks: block editor (links, images, video, music, text, folders, social icons, forms, FAQs, testimonials, IG posts), tabs and folders, hero link, pinned links, descriptions under links; import from Instagram handle or Linktree; Starter $5/mo, Pro $12/mo [R, partly vendor blog] — [Liinks](https://www.liinks.co/); [Liinks blog](https://blog.liinks.co/link-in-bio-but-make-it-money-7-underused-liinks-features-that-quietly-boost-your-revenue)
- Komi: "6 link layout styles", drag-and-drop blocks, Instagram grid mirror [R, competitor comparison, unverified] — [alinkinbio comparison](https://alinkinbio.com/komi/linktree)
- Campsite.bio: 4 link layouts, ~10 fonts per comparison table [R]; Lnk.Bio list and grid layouts, carousel on paid "Unique" tier [R] — [alinkinbio](https://alinkinbio.com/biolink/campsite); [LinkInBioTools Lnk.Bio](https://linkinbiotools.com/lnk-bio/)
- Stan Store: single-page storefront; no multiple pages, no drag-and-drop sections, no custom layouts, no countdowns, no custom domain per reviewers; all stores look similar [R] — [SchoolMaker](https://schoolmaker.com/blog/stan-store-review); [Foundry](https://www.builtbyfoundry.io/blog/stan-store-review-2026-is-it-worth-it)
- Carrd: general one-page site builder, so any layout (including hand-built bento grid) is possible but high learning curve [R] — [Liink](https://liink.is/blog/best-link-in-bio-tools); [ManyLinks](https://www.manylinks.io/bento-alternative)

### Inferences
- The market splits into five layout models: vertical list with featured upgrade (Linktree, Beacons, Komi, Lnk.Bio), tiles/grid (Bento clones, Later), swipe cards (Milkshake), multi-page/tabs (Hopp, Beacons, Liinks), free canvas (Carrd, Taplink Custom block). A WordPress plugin can offer all via a block-based layout engine; two-column desktop layouts and desktop-vs-mobile differences were not documented by any source found.
- Bento's mixed-size tile grid is now an open niche (shutdown Feb 2026) with only small vendors serving it.

### Gaps
- Exact Linktree Sticker Bio Boards mechanics, plan tier and header-layout list: no official announcement retrieved.
- No official description of Bento.me tile sizes, widgets or themes retrieved (Wayback not attempted); the acquisition claim comes from vendor blogs only (ManyLinks page itself did not mention a buyer).
- Two-column desktop layout / desktop rendering differences: no source found for any tool.
- Milkshake, Komi, Taplink, Liinks, Campsite, Stan official pages not fetched directly.

## Link presentation (thumbnails, featured, previews, badges, animations, icons, grouping)

### Takeaway
Linktree and Beacons both offer thumbnails, featured large-image links and attention animations, mostly gated behind paid plans on Linktree (thumbnails and Spotlight/animation need Starter+).

### Cited Findings
- Linktree: thumbnails on links, prioritize a link with Spotlight or animation, schedule links = Starter, Pro, Premium [O] — [Linktree paid features](https://linktr.ee/help/en/articles/5434140-an-overview-of-paid-features-available-on-linktree)
- Linktree thumbnail icons come from Tabler Icons; GIPHY GIFs supported as thumbnails [O] — [Linktree help](https://linktr.ee/help/articles/8580581-how-to-feature-your-links-with-a-visual-preview-on-linktree)
- Linktree "Suggested Titles" (AI, Sept 2025) writes descriptive link names for many social links [O] — [Linktree blog](https://linktr.ee/blog/smarter-design-tools)
- Beacons: crop link image to 16:9 (featured/thumbnail/banner) or 1:1 (logos/products); Link Outline toggle for busy themes; star icon animates a link; Classic mode collapsible links; Link Redirect is Pro (one active); scheduling, countdowns per link [O] — [Beacons Links block](https://help.beacons.ai/en/articles/4696577)
- Beacons "Full Width" option makes buttons cover full mobile width (older blog, ~1,700 days old, may be outdated) — [Beacons blog](https://beacons.ai/i/blog/customizing-your-page)
- Taplink: button animations, element sizing, scheduled timers (paid), custom HTML/CSS block (paid) [R] — [Taplink review](https://www.creatorstackclub.com/software/taplink); [Taplink blog](https://taplink.at/en/blog/what-is-taplink.html)
- Campsite: animations and custom CSS need Pro per one source (a competitor source says Pro unlocks backgrounds/buttons/fonts; conflict) [R] — [LinkInBioTools](https://linkinbiotools.com/linktree-vs-campsite/); [links.fans](https://blog.links.fans/campsite-bio-vs-links/)

### Inferences
- Pulse/shake/wobble specifics, "New"/"Sale" badges, and OG-image auto-fetch previews: not confirmed by any source retrieved. Treat as likely common but unverified.

### Gaps
- Specific animation types (pulse, shake, wobble) per tool; badges; auto-fetch of favicon/OG image; per-tool emoji handling; subtitle support except Liinks descriptions.

## Profile and header

### Takeaway
Linktree gates its richer profile styles (Hero, Banner, Cutout, Shape) and video profile images; Beacons offers 12 header options.

### Cited Findings
- Linktree profile image styles Hero, Banner, Cutout, Shape = Pro/Premium; video or AI-generated/restyled profile images = Starter+; logo title and hiding Linktree logo = Pro+; verified badge (apply) = Starter+ [O] — [Linktree paid features](https://linktr.ee/help/en/articles/5434140-an-overview-of-paid-features-available-on-linktree)
- Hero profile image: larger image, color scheme adjusts to complement it (Mar 2024) [O] — [Linktree blog](https://linktr.ee/blog/big-bold-and-visual-four-new-features-that-just-dropped-on-linktree)
- Profile video: upload or choose from 500,000+ Coverr royalty-free videos [R-ish search summary of help] — [Linktree help](https://help.linktr.ee/en/articles/9036585-add-or-change-your-linktree-profile-image)
- Beacons header block: 4 layout types, 12 options; social icons managed in Profile; toggles for share/subscribe button and Beacons logo at top/bottom [O] — [Beacons design help](https://help.beacons.ai/en/articles/4697153)

### Gaps
- Bio length limits, pronoun/location fields, social icons above vs below placement per tool, and avatar size options: not found.

## Backgrounds

### Takeaway
Image/video backgrounds are a paid feature on Linktree (Pro+); Lnk.Bio and Taplink advertise animated themes/gradients more cheaply.

### Cited Findings
- Linktree: custom image/video wallpaper = Pro/Premium; options: upload, design in Canva, royalty-free library; set via Wallpaper > Video; lightning-bolt themes Pro+; Pro gets looping backgrounds [O] — [Linktree help](https://linktr.ee/help/en/articles/5434177-how-to-add-a-custom-image-or-video-background-to-your-linktree); [Linktree blog](https://linktr.ee/blog/smarter-design-tools). Third-party warns video backgrounds slow mobile load [R] — [biotree.bio](https://biotree.bio/blog/linktree-background-image)
- Beacons: uploaded images and videos as page background; stock Unsplash images/videos on Creator Pro per plans page; gradient backgrounds and hover colors in older blog [O] — [Beacons design help](https://help.beacons.ai/en/articles/4697153); [Beacons plans](https://home.beacons.ai/plans)
- Lnk.Bio: free animated themes and custom gradient backgrounds; video wallpaper on paid Unique tier ($24.99 lifetime) [R, citing vendor page] — [LinkInBioTools](https://linkinbiotools.com/lnk-bio/)
- Taplink: static and animated themes [R] — [Taplink blog](https://taplink.at/en/blog/what-is-taplink.html). Komi: color, image, video backgrounds [R] — [alinkinbio](https://alinkinbio.com/komi/linktree). Campsite: uploaded image backgrounds [R]. Liinks: free background generator [R, vendor blog].

### Gaps
- Gradient stop counts/direction, blur/overlay controls, patterns, glass effects, generative/AI-generated backgrounds: not documented in any source retrieved.

## Buttons and typography

### Takeaway
Button shape/shadow/outline/transparency control exists at Beacons; Beacons advertises 1024+ fonts; Lnk.Bio claims vast font count; Carrd has no direct font upload.

### Cited Findings
- Beacons Block Style: shape, shadow, outline, transparency; "over 1024 different fonts" [O] — [Beacons design help](https://help.beacons.ai/en/articles/4697153)
- Linktree: buttons shape (rounded/rectangular) and color, font and text color; font and font color changes = Pro+ [O] — [Linktree help](https://linktr.ee/help/articles/8614125-customize-your-linktree-appearance); [paid features](https://linktr.ee/help/en/articles/5434140-an-overview-of-paid-features-available-on-linktree)
- Later: premade themes, custom colors, button shapes, styles, shadows [R/official help summary] — [Later help](https://help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/4409203074967-What-is-Link-in-Bio)
- Lnk.Bio claims 577 templates and 44,813 fonts on its own page (reached only via a summary; direct fetch returned 403); independent review says 368+ themes [R] — [LinkInBioTools](https://linkinbiotools.com/lnk-bio/)
- Komi: 20 fonts [R, unverified]; Campsite: Google Fonts addable [R]; Stan: reportedly one font plus background/highlight colors [R]
- Carrd: web-safe + Google Fonts API fonts; no direct upload, workaround via Embed @font-face (Pro Standard+) or Advanced Settings (Pro Plus); Widgets (Stripe, PayPal, Gumroad, Typeform, Facebook) Pro Standard+ [R, with Carrd docs] — [Carrd docs](https://carrd.com/docs/pro/features); [Starrt](https://starrt.co/blog/custom-fonts-carrd)
- Carrd plans (official): Pro Lite $9/yr (3 sites, no custom domain), Pro Standard $19/yr (10 sites, domain, forms, widgets), Pro Plus $49/yr (25 sites, advanced forms); all include no branding, video uploads, slideshows, QR codes [O] — [Carrd plans](https://carrd.com/docs/pro/plans)
- Hopp: custom fonts and domains supported [R]; Wix support article lists no font options [O]

### Gaps
- Hard vs soft shadow, hover/press effects, text-size controls, dark mode support, color picker vs fixed palette per tool: not retrieved.

## Templates and AI restyle

### Takeaway
Template counts are inconsistent across sources; Linktree's AI is "Enhance" (one-click suggested restyle, user keeps chosen changes) plus Restyle-with-AI for the avatar.

### Cited Findings
- Linktree Enhance (announced 24 Sep 2025): one click analyzes profile and suggests layouts, wallpapers, color schemes modelled on top-performing Linktrees; preview and keep selected changes. Restyle with AI converts profile photo to cartoon/sketch/3D/doodle/pixel (Starter+), original retained. Canva integration for all users; Premium yearly includes Canva Pro. 12+ new themes, fonts, layouts [O] — [Linktree blog](https://linktr.ee/blog/smarter-design-tools); [Linktree paid features](https://linktr.ee/help/en/articles/5434140-an-overview-of-paid-features-available-on-linktree)
- Linktree themes: curated or customizable base; lightning-bolt themes Pro+ [O] — [Linktree help](https://help.linktr.ee/en/articles/5434137-choose-a-theme-for-your-linktree)
- Beacons AI: helps style a page but cannot build a full page from scratch; Free = 30 daily AI credits (enough for one design), unlimited on higher plan [R] — [Taplink blog](https://taplink.at/en/blog/what-is-beacons-ai.html)
- Taplink: AI builder generates a page with content, design, structure (15 s to ~1 min depending on page); 100+ page templates and 200-400+ themes (sources conflict) [R] — [Taplink blog](https://taplink.at/en/blog/what-is-taplink.html); [Brandid](https://brandid.app/reviews/taplink/)
- Milkshake: "Looks" (templates), 16 per one source, "dozens" per another; customizable brand colors/fonts/logos/images [R] — [TheLeap](https://www.theleap.co/blog/linktree-vs-milkshake/)
- Komi: no AI page generation per competitor LOGO.com [R, competitor] — [LOGO.com](https://logo.com/guides/logo-link-in-bio-vs-komi)
- Campsite: 10+ templates [R]

### Gaps
- Template categorization (profession/mood), editability after applying, Hopp template count, Beacons AI visual output details: not found.

## Images (formats, crop, GIF, galleries, feeds)

### Takeaway

### Cited Findings
- Linktree: GIPHY GIF thumbnails, Canva/AI image generation, Image Gallery (3 vs unlimited), Instagram six-post display [O] — see sections above
- Beacons: crop to 16:9 or 1:1 in editor [O] — [Beacons Links block](https://help.beacons.ai/en/articles/4696577)
- Milkshake: GIFs and videos inside cards [R] — [TheLeap](https://www.theleap.co/blog/linktree-vs-milkshake/)
- Carrd: slideshows and video uploads on all Pro plans [O] — [Carrd plans](https://carrd.com/docs/pro/plans)

### Gaps
- Supported file formats, auto-compression, focal-point tools, TikTok feed embeds, product image grids: not found for any tool.

## What reviewers praise / best-designed

### Cited Findings
- Carrd: pick for design freedom and cheapest annual price; downsides learning curve, no built-in analytics/commerce [R] — [TrackRev/others via search](https://www.trackrev.io/blog/best-link-in-bio-tools)
- Milkshake: best mobile-first, story-like swipe experience; limited to template/card system, no desktop editor [R] — [Liink](https://liink.is/blog/best-link-in-bio-tools)
- Bento: "visual-grid leader" with polished editor and templates [R, vendor] — [ManyLinks](https://www.manylinks.io/bento-alternative)
- Campsite described as better looking out of the box than Lnk.Bio; Lnk.Bio pages criticized as too similar [R] — [Medium comparison](https://medium.com/@dmitrymoro/best-link-in-bio-tools-an-in-depth-comparison-table-4f13c4b770e2)
- Stan Store: every store looks near-identical (criticism) [R]; Later: less design flexibility than dedicated tools [R]
- Beacons: more flexible than Linktree but not website-builder level [R] — [Luvi](https://www.luvi.fans/blog/beacons-vs-linktree-for-creators/)

### Gaps
- No independent editorial (Creative Bloq, Design Shack, Zapier) design review was retrieved; nearly all comparisons are by competing vendors.

## Prioritized recommendation for a new WordPress plugin (inference, not sourced)
1. Per-link Classic vs Featured (16:9 large image card) with thumbnail upload and GIF; 1:1 thumbnails; image crop to 16:9/1:1.
2. Backgrounds: solid, gradient, image with overlay/blur, optional video (with performance caveats); these are paywalled at Linktree so free inclusion is a differentiator.
3. Bento-style mixed-size tile grid (vacated by Bento.me) plus list and a swipeable-card mode; tabs/multi-page like Hopp/Beacons.
4. Block-style controls: shape, shadow (hard/soft), outline, transparency; Google Fonts picker with local font upload (Carrd lacks direct upload).
5. Header: avatar shapes, hero/banner/cutout styles, video avatar, cover image.
6. Instagram feed-to-tiles layouts (grid, carousel, single); attention animations (pulse/shake) and badges.
7. Themes as editable presets, plus AI "enhance" suggestion step (preview-and-accept model, as Linktree).
8. Stickers (Linktree June 2026) and styled QR as later differentiators; verify sticker plan model before copying.
